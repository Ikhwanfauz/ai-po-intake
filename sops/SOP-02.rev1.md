# Purchase order verification and approval

## 1. Agreed price discrepancies

Compare each printed unit price with the agreed customer price for that SKU. Record a price discrepancy as an open issue. Ask the customer for a revised purchase order if the PDF itself carries the wrong price.

## 3. Pre-approval checks

- Verify the customer reference against the original PDF and the selected customer.
- Check each SKU and ordered quantity against the correct PDF row.
- For every corrected field, check the source page and excerpt against the recorded value.
- Confirm that no unresolved issues or pending revisions remain before approving.
