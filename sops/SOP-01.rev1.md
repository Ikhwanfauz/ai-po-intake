# Purchase order intake and revisions

## 1. Intake and customer identity

Keep the original PDF on the intake receipt. Verify the buyer's customer reference against the PDF and resolve any conflicting sender or customer context before linking it to a purchase order.

## 2. Resends and revised documents

An exact resend links to the original receipt. Compare a changed PDF with the active revision and review the differences before activating it. An unresolved candidate blocks approval. Link changes received after approval as a follow-up.
